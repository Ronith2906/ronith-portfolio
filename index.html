Corner Store

Founder · in pilot

Small retailers lose real money to expired and dead stock, but every tool that solves it assumes a modern POS and a back office. A corner store has neither. So: intake by camera — scan a barcode and photograph the expiry date, or shoot one photo of the distributor invoice. Out comes a daily plain-English action sheet with dollar figures attached: what to pull, what to mark down today, what to stop reordering. It runs beside the existing register with no POS integration, which is the only reason a store agreed to pilot it. I open-sourced the hard part as expiry-vision, a provider-agnostic library that reads expiry dates off label photos with per-read confidence scoring and an eval harness.
